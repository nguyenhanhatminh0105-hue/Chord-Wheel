Chord-Wheel gestures

Show one or two hands. MediaPipe finds 21 landmarks on each hand, and a network of 14,215 weights names the pose: fist, palm, one, peace, three, four or other. These are the poses that play Chord-Wheel. Everything runs in your browser; no image leaves your computer.
